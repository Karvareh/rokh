# D-24. The code of conduct: its words, and where a report of conduct goes

## The question

Are the words of the organization's code of conduct the owner's? And where does
a report of conduct go: to the owner's contact on their profile, as it says
now, or to an address of its own?

## Why it is asked

`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` of the organization, written when Rokh was set out in four
repositories.

## What changes once it is ruled

`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` of the organization's `.github` repository.

## Waits for

Nothing.

## Ruled when

The owner accepts or changes the words, and names where reports go.

## How it is ruled

The owner rules in rokh-docs, by a record in `rulings/` that answers D-24 and
changes the texts it changes. This record then says `status: ruled` and names
that ruling, and the missions it blocks no longer need it.
