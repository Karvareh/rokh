# D-25. Who merges, besides the owner?

## The question

Only the owner merges, in every repository. As the work grows, may others merge
some changes: the status of a mission, an erratum, the correction of a study?
And who may set the labels `claimed` and `in-review` on an issue?

## Why it is asked

`GOVERNANCE.md` of the organization: only the owner merges, in every
repository.

## What changes once it is ruled

`GOVERNANCE.md`, the `CODEOWNERS` of each repository, and the protection of
each `main`.

## Waits for

Nothing.

## Ruled when

`GOVERNANCE.md` names who merges what.

## How it is ruled

The owner rules in rokh-docs, by a record in `rulings/` that answers D-25 and
changes the texts it changes. This record then says `status: ruled` and names
that ruling, and the missions it blocks no longer need it.
