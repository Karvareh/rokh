# Contributing to rokh-docs

What is here is ruled. It is not changed by argument here; it is changed by
the owner's ruling, and argued for in rokh-lab.

- **To change what a text says,** ask in
  [rokh-lab](https://github.com/Karvareh/rokh-lab), as a question, with your
  evidence: what is asked, why, and what would change. If the owner rules, the
  change comes here with the ruling.
- **An erratum**, a sentence said more plainly, a broken reference, a typing
  error, comes as a pull request or as an issue with the erratum form. Say why
  it changes no meaning.
- **A weakness in security** that a text reveals or allows is reported
  privately, never in an issue: see the
  [security policy](https://github.com/Karvareh/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

A pull request here has one concern; its commits are in English, in the
present tense; the check of `tools/records` passes; and the owner merges it.
Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) first.
