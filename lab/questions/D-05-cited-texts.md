# D-05. The texts the comments cite: brought into a repository, or the citations replaced

## The question

The texts the comments cite (goals, packets, defects, rulings): brought into
the tree, or each citation replaced by what it rests on.

## Why it is asked

- **A-33.** About 45 comments cite texts that are not in the tree (`goal 7.1`,
  `packet C.3`, `DEFECT K1` and the like) — *evidence: C*

The findings are kept, with their evidence, in [study
0002](../studies/0002-findings-of-1.0.0/README.md); the measurements in [study
0001](../studies/0001-scale/README.md).

## What changes once it is ruled

Comments in both modules.

## Waits for

Nothing.

## Ruled when

No comment cites a text the tree does not hold.

## How it is ruled

The owner rules in rokh-docs, by a record in `rulings/` that answers D-05 and
changes the texts it changes. This record then says `status: ruled` and names
that ruling, and the missions it blocks no longer need it.
