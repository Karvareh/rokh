# D-24. The code of conduct: its words, and where a report of conduct goes

## The question

What are the words of Rokh's code of conduct, and are they the owner's? And
where does a report of conduct go: to the owner's contact on their profile,
as the draft said, or to an address of its own?

## Why it is asked

No code of conduct is published for Rokh. One was drafted when Rokh was set
out in four repositories, for the organization's shared files, and was not
published; the owner holds the draft.

## What changes once it is ruled

A code of conduct for Rokh, and where it is kept.

## Waits for

Nothing.

## Ruled when

The owner accepts or changes the words, and names where reports go.

## How it is ruled

The owner rules by a record in `docs/rulings/` that answers D-24 and
changes the texts it changes. This record then says `status: ruled` and names
that ruling, and the missions it blocks no longer need it.
