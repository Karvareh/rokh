# D-16. Seeds that carry what they rest on

## The question

Seeds that carry what they rest on and a checkpoint for the rest (contract 4.7
S2, §P2.5).

## Why it is asked

- **S-11.** A seed holds every system event before it: 3 + 4n events for the
  n-th of a line; a lineage's memory grows as the square, and some 7,000 seeds
  in a line cannot be opened on the host — *evidence: M (§M11), X (§X4)*

The findings are kept, with their evidence, in [study
0002](../studies/0002-findings-of-1.0.0/README.md); the measurements in [study
0001](../studies/0001-scale/README.md).

## What changes once it is ruled

Contract 4.7.

## Waits for

- [**D-12**](D-12-checkpoint-opening.md): May an opening rest on a checkpoint
  the owner signed?

## Ruled when

The ruling.

## How it is ruled

The owner rules by a record in `docs/rulings/` that answers D-16 and
changes the texts it changes. This record then says `status: ruled` and names
that ruling, and the missions it blocks no longer need it.
