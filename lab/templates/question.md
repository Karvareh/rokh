# D-99. What is asked, in a few words

## The question

What the owner is asked to rule, and the answers that are open.

## Why it is asked

The findings, texts or measurements that raise it, with their evidence.

## What changes once it is ruled

The texts, the code and the documents a ruling would change.

## What it blocks in work/

The missions that wait for it, also named in `blocks`; or nothing.

## Waits for

What must be known or ruled first; or *Nothing.*

## Ruled when

What the ruling must say for this question to be answered.

## How it is ruled

The owner rules by a record in `docs/rulings/` that answers this question and
changes the texts it changes. This record then says
`status: ruled` and names that ruling, and the missions it blocks no longer
need it.
