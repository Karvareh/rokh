# Versions of the texts

Every version of the texts, with the SHA-256 of each. Source names the version
it is measured against in `rokh/texts/texts.tsv`, by the same digests.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-01

ba7e695), unchanged byte for byte, moved here as their home. In rokh they were
`rokh/رساله.md`, `rokh/without-consensus.md`, `rokh/conformance.md` and
`rokh/contracts/v1.md`, and their history up to the move is there.
